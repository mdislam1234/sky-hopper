Delete your account

Permanent account and game-data deletion

Deleting your account permanently removes your Sky Hopper account and account-linked game progress. This cannot be undone.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Sky Hopper and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation and choose DELETE ACCOUNT.

The app waits for server confirmation, signs out the deleted account, and lets you decide whether to create a new, clean Guest account.

Request deletion without the app

If you uninstalled Sky Hopper or cannot access the app, email AziTech Studio support. You do not need to reinstall the game.

Email account-deletion support

Include enough non-secret account information for support to identify and verify the account. Never send a password, access token, OAuth code, or provider token.

Data that is deleted

  • the Supabase authentication account and linked Google identity, if present;
  • the Sky Hopper profile, display information, and coin balance;
  • saved scores, runs, and leaderboard-linked records;
  • owned and selected skins;
  • missions, achievements, streaks, and Daily Challenge progress;
  • rewarded-ad reward records and other account-linked game progress.

Deleting Sky Hopper data does not delete your Google Account. Sky Hopper does not intentionally retain a separate copy of account-linked game records after account deletion.
