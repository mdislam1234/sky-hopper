Sky Hopper Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Sky Hopper is a game by the Sky Hopper project. This policy explains the information used when you sign in, play, and choose to view advertising in the Android app.

Information we process

When you use Google sign-in, authentication is handled by Google and Supabase. Sky Hopper stores your account identifier, display name, avatar URL, game scores, gameplay progress, virtual coin balance, unlocked cosmetics, and related timestamps. The game does not ask for your password and does not store Google OAuth tokens in its public game tables.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for consent and provides privacy options in the app settings. The Web version does not load mobile ads.

How information is used

Game information is used to restore your profile, save runs, calculate rewards, show progression and leaderboards, prevent duplicate rewards, and operate the service. Advertising information is used by Google according to the choices available in its consent flow.

Sharing and service providers

Sky Hopper uses Google for authentication and advertising, Supabase for authentication and game data, Vercel for Web hosting, and the relevant app store for distribution. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Sky Hopper does not sell your game profile data.

Retention and security

Game records are kept while your account is active or as needed to run and protect the service. Database access controls restrict private rows to their account owner, and coin rewards are calculated by authenticated server functions. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may decline an optional rewarded ad and continue playing. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, open Settings in the Android app and choose Privacy Options. You may also use your device or Google account advertising controls. Contact the project maintainer to request access, correction, or deletion of your account data.

Children

Sky Hopper is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum digital-consent age in their region. Do not use the service if you are below the applicable age without authorization from a parent or guardian.

Policy changes and contact

This page will be updated when the data practices change. For privacy requests, use the support contact listed on the Sky Hopper store listing or repository.
